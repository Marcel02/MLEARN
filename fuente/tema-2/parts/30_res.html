MLEARN · Tema 2 · Decision theoryDuda, Hart & Stork, caps. 2–3 · diap. 1–85

1 · Bayes y MAP (d. 6–16)

Bayes (d. 7)\(\Pr(\omega_j|\bx)=\frac{f(\bx|\omega_j)\Pr(\omega_j)}{f(\bx)},\quad f(\bx)=\sum_if(\bx|\omega_i)\Pr(\omega_i)\) · posterior = verosimilitud × prior / evidencia
MAP (d. 13–15)\(\hat\omega=\arg\max_i f(\bx|\omega_i)\Pr(\omega_i)\) · minimiza \(\Pr(e)\) · \(\Pr(e|\bx)=1-\max_i\Pr(\omega_i|\bx)\) · priors iguales → ML
Error, 2 clases (d. 17)\(\Pr(e)=\Pr(\omega_1)\int_{\mathcal R_2}f_1\,d\bx+\Pr(\omega_2)\int_{\mathcal R_1}f_2\,d\bx\)

2 · Riesgo mínimo (MBR) (d. 17–22)

Riesgo\(\mathcal R=\sum_{i,j}\pi_{ij}\Pr(\omega_i|\omega_j)\Pr(\omega_j)\) · \(\pi_{ij}\): decidir \(\omega_i\) siendo \(\omega_j\) · \(\Pr(\omega_i|\omega_j)=\int_{\mathcal R_i}f(\bx|\omega_j)\,d\bx\)
Regla\(\mathcal C(\omega_i|\bx)=\sum_j\pi_{ij}\Pr(\omega_j|\bx)\), \(\hat\omega=\arg\min_i\mathcal C(\omega_i|\bx)\) · costes 0-1 → MAP
Binaria (d. 20)\(\frac{\Pr(\omega_1|\bx)}{\Pr(\omega_2|\bx)}\underset{\omega_2}{\overset{\omega_1}{\gtrless}}\gamma=\frac{\pi_{12}-\pi_{22}}{\pi_{21}-\pi_{11}}\iff\Pr(\omega_1|\bx)\gtrless\frac{\gamma}{1+\gamma}\) · cociente de posteriores

3 · Neyman-Pearson y minimax (d. 23–28)

Probabilidades\(P_D=\int_{\mathcal R_2}f(\bx|\omega_2)\,d\bx,\quad P_{FA}=\int_{\mathcal R_2}f(\bx|\omega_1)\,d\bx\)
NP (d. 25)\(\max P_D\) con \(P_{FA}\le\alpha\): \(\frac{f(\bx|\omega_2)}{f(\bx|\omega_1)}\underset{\omega_1}{\overset{\omega_2}{\gtrless}}\gamma\) · Lagrangiano \(\int_{\mathcal R_2}(f_2-\gamma f_1)\,d\bx+\gamma\alpha\) · sin priors
1D gaussiano\(\mathcal N(0,1)\) frente a \(\mathcal N(D',1)\): \(\gamma'=Q^{-1}(\alpha)\), \(P_D=Q(\gamma'-D')\) · \(\alpha=0{,}05,\ D'=2\Rightarrow P_D=0{,}639\)
Minimax (d. 27)\((\pi_{11}-\pi_{22})+(\pi_{21}-\pi_{11})\int_{\mathcal R_2}f_1-(\pi_{12}-\pi_{22})\int_{\mathcal R_1}f_2=0\) · costes 0-1: \(P_{FA}=P_{\text{miss}}\)

4 · Gaussianas y discriminantes (d. 31–43)

DiscriminantesMBR: \(-\mathcal C(\omega_i|\bx)\) · MAP: \(\Pr(\omega_i|\bx)\) · NP: \(f(\bx|\omega_i)\) · decidir el mayor; vale cualquier función creciente
Gaussiana (d. 33)\(f(\bx)=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{d/2}|\bC|^{1/2}}e^{-\frac12(\bx-\bmu)^T\bC^{-1}(\bx-\bmu)}\) · \(\Pr(x>\mu+2\sigma)=Q(2)=2{,}28\,\%\)
Lineal (d. 34–35)\(\bz=\bA^T\bx\): \(\bmu_z=\bA^T\bmu\), \(\bC_z=\bA^T\bC\bA\) · blanqueado \(\bA=\bU\bLambda^{-1/2}\), \(\bLambda^{-1/2}=\mathrm{diag}(1/\sqrt{\lambda_i})\)
QDA (d. 39)\(g_i=-\frac12(\bx-\bmu_i)^T\bC_i^{-1}(\bx-\bmu_i)-\frac12\ln|\bC_i|+\ln\Pr(\omega_i)\)
Frontera (d. 40–41)\(\bx^T\bA\bx+\bv^T\bx+e=0\), \(\bA=\frac12(\bC_j^{-1}-\bC_i^{-1})\) · autovalores de \(\bA\): mismo signo → elipse; opuesto → hipérbola; uno nulo → parábola; \(\bA=\mathbf 0\) → recta

5 · Casos particulares (d. 44–50)

LDA (\(\bC\) común) (d. 48)\(\bw^T(\bx-\bx_0)=0,\ \bw=\bC^{-1}(\bmu_i-\bmu_j),\ \bx_0=\frac12(\bmu_i+\bmu_j)-\frac{\ln[\Pr(\omega_i)/\Pr(\omega_j)]}{(\bmu_i-\bmu_j)^T\bC^{-1}(\bmu_i-\bmu_j)}(\bmu_i-\bmu_j)\)
MBR con \(\bC\) comúnigual \(\bw\); en \(\bx_0\), \(\Pr(\omega_i)\to(\pi_{ji}-\pi_{ii})\Pr(\omega_i)\) y \(\Pr(\omega_j)\to(\pi_{ij}-\pi_{jj})\Pr(\omega_j)\) · 3 clases con costes generales: frontera curva
Diagonal / isotrópica (d. 47)\(g_i=\ln\Pr(\omega_i)-\frac12\sum_j\frac{(x_j-\mu_{ij})^2}{\sigma_j^2}\) · \(\bC=\sigma^2\mathbf I\) y priors iguales: media euclídea más cercana (mediatriz)
Binarias (d. 50)\(h(\bx)=\sum_ix_i\ln\frac{p_i(1-q_i)}{q_i(1-p_i)}+\sum_i\ln\frac{1-p_i}{1-q_i}+\ln\frac{\Pr(\omega_1)}{\Pr(\omega_2)}\) (lineal) · \(p_i=1-q_i=p\): mayoría, \(P_e=\sum_{k\le(d-1)/2}\binom dkp^k(1-p)^{d-k}\)

6 · Estimación ML y pocos datos (d. 51–65)

ML (d. 54)\(\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}_i=\arg\max\sum_k\ln f(\bx_{k,i}|\boldsymbol\theta_i)\) · asintóticamente insesgado y eficiente
Estimadores (d. 59–60)\(\hat\bmu=\frac1N\sum\bx_k\), \(\hat\bC=\frac1N\sum(\bx_k-\hat\bmu)(\bx_k-\hat\bmu)^T\) (\(E\{\hat\sigma^2\}=\frac{N-1}N\sigma^2\)) · \(\hat p_k=\frac1N\sum_jx_{k,j}\) · priors \(N_k/N\)
LDA / QDA (d. 61)LDA: \(\hat\bC=\frac1N\sum_kN_k\hat\bC_k\) · QDA: un \(\hat\bC_k\) por clase · singular si \(N_k\le d\)
RDA (d. 63)\(\hat\bC_k(\alpha)=\alpha\hat\bC_k+(1-\alpha)\hat\bC\) (1 = QDA, 0 = LDA) · \((1-\gamma)\hat\bC_k(\alpha)+\gamma\hat\sigma^2\mathbf I\), \(\hat\sigma^2=\frac1d\tr\)
Naive Bayes (d. 64–65)\(f(\bx|\omega)=\prod_jf(x_j|\omega)\) · Bernoulli → lineal · gaussiano → QDA diagonal (\(d\) parámetros frente a \(\frac{d(d+1)}2\))
