MLEARN · Tema 3 · Feature selectionDuda, Hart & Stork, cap. 3.7–3.8 · diap. 1–43

1 · Dimensionalidad

Covarianza estimada (d. 5)\(\hat{\bC}=\frac1N\sum_{i}(\bx_i-\hat{\bmu})(\bx_i-\hat{\bmu})^T,\quad \rank(\hat{\bC})\le\min(d,N-1)\Rightarrow\) singular si \(N\le d\)
Parámetros de \(\bC\)\(\frac{d(d+1)}{2}\) por clase (crece como \(d^2\))
Dos gaussianas, misma \(\bC\), equiprobables (d. 8–9)\(\Pr(e)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{r/2}^\infty e^{-u^2/2}du=Q(r/2),\quad r^2=(\bmu_1-\bmu_2)^T\bC^{-1}(\bmu_1-\bmu_2)\)
Características independientes\(r^2=\sum_{i=1}^d\left(\frac{\mu_{i,1}-\mu_{i,2}}{\sigma_i}\right)^2=\sum_iD_i'^2\); relevantes y \(d\to\infty\Rightarrow\Pr(e)\to0\) (parámetros conocidos)
Valores de \(Q\)\(Q(0)=0{,}5\quad Q(0{,}5)=0{,}309\quad Q(1)=0{,}159\quad Q(2)=0{,}023\quad Q(3)=0{,}0013\)

2 · Transformación lineal (d. 13)

Reducción\(\bz_k=\bW^T(\bx_k-\balpha),\quad \bx\in\mathbb{R}^d,\ \bz\in\mathbb{R}^{d'},\ \bW\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d'},\ d'<d\)
Reconstrucción (d. 14)\(\hat{\bx}_k=\bW\bz_k+\balpha,\qquad \be_k=\bx_k-\hat{\bx}_k\)
Componente \(j\)\(z_{k,j}=\bw_j^T(\bx_k-\balpha)\) = coordenada sobre \(\bw_j\) si \(\|\bw_j\|=1\)

3 · PCA (d. 14–25)

Criterio\(J=\sum_{k=1}^N\|\bW\bz_k+\balpha-\bx_k\|_2^2\) (mínimo)
Solución (d. 15)\(\balpha=\bm=\frac1N\sum_k\bx_k,\quad \bS=\sum_k(\bx_k-\bm)(\bx_k-\bm)^T,\quad \bS\bw_j=\lambda_j\bw_j,\quad \bW=[\bw_1\cdots\bw_{d'}]\) con los \(d'\) mayores \(\lambda_j\)
\(\bS\) y covarianza\(\bS=(N-1)\hat{\bC}_{\text{insesgada}}=N\hat{\bC}_{\text{MV}}\): mismos autovectores
Propiedades de \(\bS\) (p. 18)\(\bS=\bS^T\), \(\bv^T\bS\bv\ge0\), \(\lambda_j\ge0\), autovectores ortogonales, \(\lambda_j=\sum_kz_{k,j}^2\), \(\bW^T\bS\bW=\mathrm{diag}(\lambda_j)\)
\(\bz_k\) óptimo, \(\bW\) cualquiera (d. 17)\(\bz_k=(\bW^T\bW)^{-1}\bW^T(\bx_k-\bm),\quad \hat J=-\sum_k(\bx_k-\bm)^T\bP(\bx_k-\bm)\)
Proyector (d. 18)\(\bP=\bW(\bW^T\bW)^{-1}\bW^T=\bU_L\bU_L^T\) (simétrico, idempotente) ⇒ se toma \(\bW^T\bW=\mathbf I\)
Traza (d. 19)\(\sum_k(\bx_k-\bm)^T\bW\bW^T(\bx_k-\bm)=\tr(\bW^T\bS\bW)=\sum_{i=1}^{d'}\bw_i^T\bS\bw_i\)
Lagrangiano\(\sum_i\bw_i^T\bS\bw_i+\sum_i\lambda_i(E-\bw_i^T\bw_i)\Rightarrow\bS\bw_i=\lambda_i\bw_i\) (más ortogonalidad entre columnas)
Error mínimo (d. 20)\(J_{\min}=\sum_k\|(\mathbf I-\bW\bW^T)(\bx_k-\bm)\|^2=\tr(\bS)-\tr(\bW^T\bS\bW)=\sum_{i=d'+1}^d\lambda_i\)
Hiperelipsoide (d. 23)\((\bx-\bm)^T\bS^{-1}(\bx-\bm)=K\iff\sum_i\frac{z_i^2}{K\lambda_i}=1\): semiejes \(\sqrt{K\lambda_i}\) en las direcciones \(\bw_i\)
Restar la media (d. 24)\(\sum_k\bx_k\bx_k^T=\bS+N\bm\bm^T\)
Eigenfaces (d. 22)\(\tilde{\bx}=\bW\bz+\bm=\bm+\sum_{j=1}^{d'}z_j\bw_j\)

4 · MDA (d. 26–37)

Medias (d. 28)\(\bm_i=\frac{1}{N_i}\sum_{\bx\in D_i}\bx,\qquad \bm=\frac1N\sum_{\bx}\bx=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^cN_i\bm_i\)
Dispersiones (d. 30)\(\bS_{C,i}=\sum_{\bx\in D_i}(\bx-\bm_i)(\bx-\bm_i)^T,\quad \bS_C=\sum_i\bS_{C,i},\quad \bS_B=\sum_iN_i(\bm_i-\bm)(\bm_i-\bm)^T\)
Descomposición\(\bS_T=\sum_{\bx}(\bx-\bm)(\bx-\bm)^T=\bS_C+\bS_B\) (cruzados nulos: \(\sum_{\bx\in D_i}(\bx-\bm_i)=\mathbf 0\))
En el espacio de \(\bz\) (d. 31)\(\bz=\bW^T\bx:\quad \bS_C\to\bW^T\bS_C\bW,\quad \bS_B\to\bW^T\bS_B\bW\)
Volumen del elipsoide\(V=V_d|\bS|^{1/2}K^{d/2},\quad V_d=\frac{\pi^{d/2}}{(d/2)!}\) (par), \(\frac{2^d((d-1)/2)!}{d!}\pi^{(d-1)/2}\) (impar)
Criterio (d. 33)\(\bW=\arg\max_{\bW}\dfrac{|\bW^T\bS_B\bW|}{|\bW^T\bS_C\bW|}\quad(=D^2,\ D=\text{cociente de volúmenes})\)
Solución\(\bS_B\bw_j=\sigma_j\bS_C\bw_j\iff\bS_C^{-1}\bS_B\bW=\bW\bSigma\); los \(d'\) mayores \(\sigma_j\)
Valor (d. 34)\(\frac{|\bW^T\bS_B\bW|}{|\bW^T\bS_C\bW|}=|\bSigma|=\prod_{i=1}^{d'}\sigma_i\)
Dimensión máxima\(d'\le\min(d,c-1)\): \(\rank(\bS_B)\le c-1\), \(\rank(\bS_C)=d\) (exige \(N-c\ge d\))
Dos clases (ej. 4)\(\bS_B=\frac{N_1N_2}{N}(\bm_1-\bm_2)(\bm_1-\bm_2)^T,\quad \bw\propto\bS_C^{-1}(\bm_1-\bm_2)\)
\(\bS_T\) en lugar de \(\bS_C\)mismos \(\bw_j\); autovalores \(\frac{\sigma_j}{1+\sigma_j}\)
Por grupos (d. 35)\(K\) grupos de características ⇒ \(d'=(c-1)K\)
Clasificador en \(\bz\) (d. 37)\(\bmu_i=\bW^T\bm_i,\qquad \bC_i=\frac{1}{N_i}\bW^T\bS_{C,i}\bW\)

5 · Ejercicios clave

Ej. 1\(\bw_i^T\bS_C\bw_j=\delta_{ij}\Rightarrow\tilde{\bS}_C=\mathbf I,\ \tilde{\bS}_B=\mathrm{diag}(\lambda_j),\ J=\prod\lambda_i\); invariante a \(\by=\mathbf Q\mathbf D\bW^T\bx\)
Ej. 2 y 5\(\max_{\|\bw\|=1}\bw^T\bS\bw=\lambda_{\max}\); con \(\bW^T\bW=\mathbf I\): \(\max\tr(\bW^T\bS\bW)=\sum_{i\le d'}\lambda_i\)
Ej. 3\(\sum_i(\bm_i-\bm)=\mathbf 0\Rightarrow\rank(\bS_B)\le c-1\); \(\rank(\bM\bM^T)=\rank(\bM)\)
Ej. 4\(\nabla\frac{\bw^T\bS_B\bw}{\bw^T\bS_C\bw}=\mathbf 0\Rightarrow\bS_B\bw=\lambda\bS_C\bw\), \(\lambda\) = el propio cociente
